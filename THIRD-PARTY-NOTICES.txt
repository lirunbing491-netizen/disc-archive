Replacement / modification:
The 7-Zip DLL is loaded dynamically. In the portable distribution, users may
replace native/x64/7z.dll or native/x86/7z.dll with their own compatible build,
matching the process architecture and the upstream 7-Zip interface. In this
source checkout, the original dependency is in vendor/7zip/x64/7z.dll or
vendor/7zip/x86/7z.dll. Keep a backup and restart the application after replacing
the distributed native DLL. No signature/hash enforcement prevents users
from running a compatible modified library. Reverse engineering for debugging
modifications to this LGPL library is permitted. This notice does not restrict
the rights granted by the LGPL. The application's own license is separate.

Build instructions and sources are in the exact source archive DOC/readme.txt;
the Windows binaries can be built with Visual Studio and the supplied makefiles.
The upstream binaries distributed here have not been modified.

Redistribution checklist:
Include this notice, vendor/7zip/LICENSE-7zip.txt,
vendor/7zip/COPYING-LGPL-2.1.txt, vendor/7zip/unRarLicense.txt,
